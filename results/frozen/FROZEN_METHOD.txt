  - CrySyS labels: only traces that passed every check of crysys_labels.py (label_report.csv, passed = True); frame labels from labels/<trace>.npy, never the JSON markers; window = attack if it holds at least one attack frame; uncertain windows are left out of every window-level result and count as attack windows for alarm episodes.
